## Rating: SFW (all audiences)

- Romance, attraction, kisses and affection are welcome and may be written warmly.
- Sexual situations are never depicted. Where the comic shows or implies them, close the
  door: cut away, imply with a time skip or a quiet image, and move on. Nudity is
  mentioned without erotic detail or avoided.
- Violence may be intense but not gratuitous: convey impact and consequence without
  lingering on gore or anatomical detail.
- Language may be rough where the characters would be rough, but keep slurs and crude
  sexual vocabulary out of the narration.
- Never lose the plot or emotional point of the scene while softening it.
