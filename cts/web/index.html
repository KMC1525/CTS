        Context window

        How much text the model can hold at once. Larger windows let CTS feed more of the story-so-far to each page (better continuity) but need more memory with local models. 16K-32K is a good start; CTS needs at least 8K.

        About

        Closing this tab does not stop CTS.

        Adults only

        The NSFW edition writes mature and sexual material between adult characters where the comic shows or implies it. Pages that may involve minors are always written without sexual content.

        Hosted AI providers may decline NSFW pages; CTS then falls back to the SFW version of that page. Local models are the most reliable choice.
